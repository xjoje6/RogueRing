# Changelog

## 0.8.0 (2026-10-24)

A full remake since the prealpha. It replaces the old page (mod 11193).

### New
- **Co-op** with Seamless Co-op (install it separately). Open or join a room from the mod's menu, everyone
  picks a character and the host sets the Ascension. One shared map with votes, rewards for each player,
  and a fallen player gets back up when a teammate holds Y, like in Nightreign.
- In co-op you never fight a lone enemy: pairs, waves and packs. Every run draws new pairs from the
  enemies of that act and tier, so no two runs look the same.
- Enemies have 80 % more HP for each player after the first.
- Four characters: **Monk** (heals on every hit, quick step instead of a roll), **Samurai** (deflects any
  blow, Sekiro style), **Bulwark** (nothing staggers him, two-handed by default) and **Summoner** (starts
  every fight with a spirit summon). Each has an outfit, blessings and three starting weapons.
- Three acts, with the lord of each act at the end.
- Bosses from the Souls games that Nightreign brought back: Nameless King, Dancer of the Boreal Valley,
  Knight Artorias, Smelter Demon, Centipede Demon, Gaping Dragon and Duke's Dear Freja.
- Many more enemies. Every enemy of the game that can fight in the colosseum is in the lists; the ones
  left out have a reason (they fly, don't fit or don't fight).
- **Big arena.** Large enemies and large groups fight on Placidusax's platform, the rest in the
  colosseum. The split is automatic, by the size of what comes out at once.
- Two-phase bosses keep their second phase: Radahn, Maliketh, Godfrey, Midra, Messmer and Malenia, and the
  Elden Beast after Radagon. Godrick grafts the dragon head and Mohg casts his Nihil, as in their arenas.
- The game's own boss bars, up to three at once, each with its enemy's name, plus waves, mutators and
  the ailments building up.
- **Ascension**, 10 levels, much harder than before. A screen lists every effect and the mutator of the run.
- **Mythic blessings**, a rarity above Rare: big effects with a price you see before picking them.
- Any weapon can show up for any character. Weapons of your own style show up more often; the rest get a
  damage boost to stay useful.
- The mod has its own main menu, pause menu and loading screens. The game's title screen is never shown.
- Characters screen with the character in 3D, their outfit and weapons.
- **Spanish.** With the game in Spanish, every screen, blessing, event and name is in Spanish.
- Combat pause shows every blessing at work, with its progress and what it does.
- Graces offer a third choice, **Temper**: one of your blessings goes up a level.
- Combat music: the boss's own theme or a field boss theme. It rises when the enemy drops below half HP.
- New players get a template save, so nobody has to create a character.

### Changed
- Weapons level up with each act: +8, +17 and +25 (unique weapons +3, +7 and +10).
- Full FP at the start of every fight.
- Flasks heal all your HP and only refill at graces.
- Enemy HP follows one curve by act and tier, measured fight by fight.
- Blessing damage is measured in your own hits, so it keeps up with your weapon. Tempest and tempered
  Will-o'-the-Wisps hit a bit softer.
- Rewards and shops never offer what you already carry.
- Enemies getting up after a critical or a fall can be hit, without being knocked back on their feet.
- Weapons have no stat requirements.

### Removed
- The Y + RT abilities and the chaos augments at the start of each act (their best effects are now
  mythic blessings).
- The Blood Scion, Brawler and Ratling characters.

### Fixed
- Runes from the previous run no longer wait on the colosseum floor.
- Some enemies stood still if you led them to the edge of the arena.
- Critical hits no longer split the enemy's reaction from your animation.

## 0.1.0-prealpha (2026-10-01)

First public version: three characters, 125 blessings, chaos augments, two acts with seeded maps,
26 events, merchants and graces, 10 Ascension levels, a blessing compendium and an end of run graph.
