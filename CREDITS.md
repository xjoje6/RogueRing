# Credits

RogueRing is made by Mionel Lessi, with the help of AI tools (Claude).

## Tools and libraries

- [me3](https://github.com/garyttierney/me3) loads the mod into the game.
- [fromsoftware-rs](https://github.com/vswarte/fromsoftware-rs) (MIT NON-AI or Apache-2.0 NON-AI: no use for training AI models): game structures and functions used by the DLL.
- [hudhook](https://github.com/veeenu/hudhook) and [imgui-rs](https://github.com/imgui-rs/imgui-rs): the in-game screens.
- SoulsFormats and Andre, through [Smithbox](https://github.com/vawser/Smithbox): reading and writing the game files when building the mod.
- [soulsmods/EldenRingHKS](https://github.com/soulsmods/EldenRingHKS): the community decompile of the player behavior script, which the mod extends.
- [Seamless Co-op](https://www.nexusmods.com/eldenring/mods/510) by LukeYui: co-op runs on it (not included).

## Assets

- Bloodborne and Sekiro animations from Blubber's [Player Animation Repositories Collection](https://www.nexusmods.com/eldenring/mods/3027), free to use with credit and not for paid mods.
- Fonts: [EB Garamond](https://github.com/octaviopardo/EBGaramond12) and [Libre Caslon Text](https://github.com/thundernixon/Libre-Caslon), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licenses in data/fuentes).
- The Souls bosses from ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN (Nameless King, Artorias, the Dancer and the rest) are ported from that game's own files with the mod's tools. Slendahmon's Nightreign boss ports on Nexus showed it could be done.
- Item icons, loading screen art, map icons and menu pieces are taken from ELDEN RING. ELDEN RING and ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN belong to FromSoftware and Bandai Namco. RogueRing is a free fan mod and is not affiliated with them.
